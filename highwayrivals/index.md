**Effective date: 6 September 2026**

This policy explains what happens to information when you play **HIGHWAY
RIVALS** ("the game"), a mobile game published by Muhammad Hazki Hariowibowo,
trading as MHz Games ("we", "us").

---

## The short version

- The game has **no accounts, no login and no servers of its own**, and never
  asks who you are.
- Your best distance, your cars and your settings are saved **only on your own
  device**, and may be included in your device's own cloud backup if you use
  one. We cannot see them either way.
- The game sends a **small amount of anonymous usage data** — that a run
  started, how it ended, which gear you reached, whether you took a rewarded
  ad — under a random ID that is not linked to you.
- The game shows ads supplied by **Google AdMob**. Google receives information
  from your device in order to serve those ads.
- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland,
  you are asked to make a choice about ads the first time you play, and you can
  change it at any time from **Settings → MANAGE AD CONSENT**.
- The game is **not directed to children under 13**.

## 1. Information the game collects

**Nothing that identifies you.** The game has no accounts and never asks for
your name, email address, contacts, location or anything similar.

### Usage data

To understand how the game is played, it sends a small number of **anonymous
usage events**: the app was opened; a run started, ended or was restarted; you
shifted up a gear or dropped one; a rewarded ad was offered, taken or shown; an
interruption ad was shown; the garage was opened; a car was previewed, bought,
selected or repainted; a purchase was attempted and whether it went through;
the profile was reset.

Those events describe the driving and nothing else — how far the run got, which
gear it reached, how it ended, and the in-game Stacks it paid. Stacks are not
money: they cannot be cashed out and exist only inside the game.

This data is received and stored for us by **Unity Gaming Services (UGS)
Analytics**, part of Unity Technologies, under its own privacy policy:
<https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy>. Alongside
each event the Unity SDK sends what it collects by default: the app version,
the platform and operating system version, the device model, the country the
request arrived from, and a **random installation ID** the SDK generates the
first time the game runs. That ID is not derived from you or from your
advertising identifier, and we cannot connect it to you. Uninstalling the game
discards it.

We use this for one thing: knowing where players stop. A game whose whole
subject is a difficulty ramp cannot be tuned from the desk it was made on, and
these events are how the ramp gets fixed for everyone.

The game also reports **uncaught errors** — the type of error and where in the
code it happened. These carry no information about you.

### Data kept on your device

The game writes one small file to its private app storage so it can remember
your progress between sessions:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your best distance and how many runs you have made | So a record survives closing the game |
| Stacks, the cars you own and the one you are driving | So you keep what you bought |
| The sound setting | So the game opens the way you left it |
| Whether you own the ad-free upgrade | So you are not shown ads you paid to remove |
| Your recorded consent choices | So you are not asked the same question repeatedly |

That file is in the app's private storage area and is readable only by the
game. **We never receive it and have no way to access it** — the game does not
upload it anywhere.

**It may, however, be included in your device's own cloud backup.** If Android
automatic backup or iCloud Backup is switched on, your device may copy this
app's saved data into your personal cloud account, exactly as it does for other
apps. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Google or Apple under their
own policies, not by us. You can turn it off in your device's settings.

Uninstalling the game deletes the file from your device. A copy may remain in
your own backup until that backup is replaced or you delete it.

## 2. Information collected by advertising

HIGHWAY RIVALS shows advertisements supplied by **Google AdMob**. To do this,
the Google Mobile Ads SDK is included in the game and communicates directly
with Google's servers.

Google may receive and process information including your device's advertising
identifier, IP address, device type and operating system, general location
derived from your IP address, and how you interacted with an ad. **We do not
receive this information.** We see only aggregate, anonymous reports — for
example, how many ads were shown in total. The usage data described in section
1 is never shared with Google, and the random installation ID is never sent
to it.

How Google uses this data is governed by Google's own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy — <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services —
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

Whether the ads you see are personalised depends on the choice you make in the
consent prompt described in section 4, and on your device's own settings.

There are two kinds of ad in the game, and they behave differently. The
**interruption ad** appears occasionally after a crash. The **rewarded ad** is
never shown unless you ask for it — it is the one that revives a run, or
doubles what a run paid.

## 3. Purchases

If you buy the ad-free upgrade, the purchase is handled entirely by **Google
Play** (or **Apple's App Store** on iOS). Your payment details go to them, never
to us — we never see or store your card number, billing address, or any other
payment information. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase completed,
which the game uses to grant what you bought.

Google's and Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by their own
privacy policies.

## 4. Your choices

**If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland**, the game shows a consent form
the first time you play, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. Your
answer determines whether the ads you see are personalised.

**You can change your answer at any time** from **Settings → MANAGE AD
CONSENT** inside the game. That row appears wherever a consent choice applies
to you.

**On any device**, you can also limit ad tracking through your device's own
settings:

- **Android** — Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset or delete your
  advertising ID
- **iOS** — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy &
  Security → Apple Advertising

**To remove ads**, buy the ad-free upgrade from **Settings → REMOVE ADS**. It
removes the interruption ad after a crash. The rewarded ad stays available,
because it is a way to get something — a second chance, or double the payout —
rather than an interruption, and removing it would take something away from you
rather than give something back.

**To wipe what the game has saved about you** without uninstalling, use
**Settings → RESET PROFILE**. It clears the local save file described in
section 1.

**To stop usage data being sent**, uninstall the game. There is no separate
switch for it inside the game; the data is anonymous and is not connected to
you.

## 5. Children

HIGHWAY RIVALS is **not directed to children under 13**, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our declared
target audience with both app stores reflects this.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game,
please contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

## 6. Data retention

Usage data (section 1) is retained by Unity Gaming Services according to its
own retention schedule for the plan we use, and we keep it for as long as it is
useful for tuning the game. It is held only as events under a random ID, and we
never build profiles of individual players from it. Data stored on your device
stays there until you reset the profile, clear the app's data, or uninstall.
Data held by Google as an advertising partner is retained according to Google's
own policies, linked in section 2.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object to it.

The usage data we hold is not linked to you. It sits under a random
installation ID that the analytics SDK keeps in the app's own private storage
and never displays, so there is nothing you could send us that would identify
your events, and nothing we could use to find them. That is one fact seen from
both sides: it is why the data cannot be traced back to you, and it is why we
cannot pick your events out of it to delete them on request. Uninstalling the
game discards the ID, and nothing further is ever recorded against that
install.

To exercise a right, or to ask anything about this policy, write to the address
below.
