**Effective date: 5 October 2026**

This policy explains what happens to information when you play **Sandcastle
Tide** ("the game"), a mobile puzzle game published by Muhammad Hazki
Hariowibowo, trading as MHz Games ("we", "us").

---

## The short version

- The game has **no accounts, no login and no servers of its own**, and never
  asks who you are.
- It **sends no usage data or analytics** of any kind. The only thing in the
  game that talks to the internet is Google's advertising.
- Your level progress, your stars, your best Endless score and your sound
  setting are saved **only on your own device**, and may be included in your
  device's own cloud backup if you use one. We cannot see them either way.
- The game shows ads supplied by **Google AdMob**. Google receives information
  from your device in order to serve those ads.
- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland,
  you are asked to make a choice about ads the first time you play, and you can
  change it at any time from **Privacy options** in the main menu or the pause
  menu.
- The game is **not directed to children under 13**.
- There are **no purchases** in the game.

## 1. Information the game collects

**Nothing that identifies you.** The game has no accounts and never asks for
your name, email address, contacts, location or anything similar.

**It sends nothing to us.** There is no analytics or crash-reporting service in
the game, and it makes no network requests of its own. We do not know who plays
it, how far they got, or how they play.

### Data kept on your device

The game keeps a few small values in its private app storage so it can
remember your progress between sessions:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| The furthest level you have unlocked, and the stars you earned on each | So your progress survives closing the game |
| Your best score in Endless | So a record is kept |
| The sound setting | So the game opens the way you left it |
| A save-format number | So a future update can tell how your progress was saved |

These are in the app's private storage area and are readable only by the
game. **We never receive them and have no way to access them** — the game does
not upload them anywhere.

**They may, however, be included in your device's own cloud backup.** If
Android automatic backup or iCloud Backup is switched on, your device may copy
this app's saved data into your personal cloud account, exactly as it does for
other apps. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Google or Apple under
their own policies, not by us. You can turn it off in your device's settings.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device's settings, deletes
these values from your device. A copy may remain in your own backup until that
backup is replaced or you delete it.

## 2. Information collected by advertising

Sandcastle Tide shows advertisements supplied by **Google AdMob**. To do this,
the Google Mobile Ads SDK is included in the game and communicates directly
with Google's servers.

Google may receive and process information including your device's advertising
identifier, IP address, device type and operating system, general location
derived from your IP address, and how you interacted with an ad. **We do not
receive this information.** We see only aggregate, anonymous reports — for
example, how many ads were shown in total.

How Google uses this data is governed by Google's own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy — <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services —
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

Whether the ads you see are personalised depends on the choice you make in the
consent prompt described in section 3, and on your device's own settings.

There are two kinds of ad in the game, and they behave differently. An
**interruption ad** appears now and then between levels or when you start a
new Endless run, never during play and never more often than every minute or
so. A **rewarded ad** is never shown unless you ask for it — it is the one
behind the **AD** tag on a button: a hint, an extra rewind after a lost tide,
carrying on an Endless run after a tower falls, or skipping a level.

## 3. Your choices

**If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland**, the game shows a consent form
the first time you play, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. Your
answer determines whether the ads you see are personalised.

**You can change your answer at any time** from **Privacy options**, in the
main menu and in the pause menu. That button appears wherever a consent choice
applies to you.

**On any device**, you can also limit ad tracking through your device's own
settings:

- **Android** — Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset or delete your
  advertising ID
- **iOS** — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy &
  Security → Apple Advertising

**To wipe what the game has saved**, clear the app's data in your device's
settings, or uninstall the game.

## 4. Children

Sandcastle Tide is **not directed to children under 13**, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our declared
target audience with the app stores reflects this.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game,
please contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

## 5. Data retention

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain. Data stored on
your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall. Data held
by Google as an advertising partner is retained according to Google's own
policies, linked in section 2.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object to it. We do not
hold any personal data about you; the game's saved progress stays on your
device, where you can delete it yourself as described in section 3. For data
Google processes to serve ads, use the controls in section 3 or Google's own
tools.

To ask anything about this policy, write to the address below.
