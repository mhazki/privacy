**Effective date: 28 September 2026**

This policy explains what happens to information when you play **Fomalhaut
Raiders** ("the game"), a mobile space shooter published by Muhammad Hazki
Hariowibowo, trading as MHz Games ("we", "us").

---

## The short version

- This version of the game **collects nothing about you**. It has no
  accounts, no login, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it
  never asks who you are.
- The game has **permission to use the internet**, because future versions
  will add ads, in-app purchases and anonymous usage analytics. This version
  does not use it: nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Before a version with ads, purchases or analytics is released, this policy
  will be updated** to say what is sent, to whom and why.
- Your progress (credits, crystals, parts, fitted mods and settings) is
  saved **only on your own device**. Your device's own cloud backup may include
  it if you use one. We can't see it either way.

## 1. Information the game collects

None. This version has no code that sends data anywhere. It does not read
your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera or any identifier on your
device. The only permission it holds is internet access, which Android grants
without asking and which this version does not use.

A future version will send anonymous usage data (for example, which missions
were flown and how they went) so we can see where the game is too hard. That
section will be added here, with the service that stores it, before the
version that sends it is released.

### Data kept on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the game stores a save on your
device. It holds your credits, crystals, unlocked parts, fitted mods and
settings. It contains nothing that identifies you. It stays on your device,
except that Android's own backup may copy it to your Google account if you
have backup turned on. That copy is handled by Google under your agreement
with Google, and we can't see it.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes the save.

## 2. Advertising

This version shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK.

A future version will show ads supplied by a third-party ad network, which will
receive information from your device in order to serve them. The network, what
it receives and the choices you have about it will be set out here before that
version is released.

## 3. Purchases

This version has no in-app purchases.

A future version will offer in-app purchases. They will be processed by Google
Play, and we will never see or store your payment details. Details will be
added here before that version is released.

## 4. Google Play

You download the game from Google Play, and Google processes information
about that download (and any crash reports your device chooses to send
through Android) under its own privacy policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>. We may see those crash reports in
aggregate, with no information that identifies you, and use them only to fix
the game.

## 5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal
information from anyone, children included.

## 6. Data retention

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete.
The save on your device lasts until you uninstall the game or clear its
storage.

## 7. Your rights

Because we collect no personal information, there's nothing we hold that we
could show you, correct or delete. If you have a question about this, write
to us at the address below.

## 8. Changes to this policy

Ads, in-app purchases and usage analytics are planned for future versions.
When a version starts to collect or send any information, this policy will be
updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
